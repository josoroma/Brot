# Brot prompt examples

Use these as starting briefs, then adapt the emotion, palette, features, and motion to the request.

## Featured multi-expression sphere

> Create a spherical 3D Brot with radial cyan–violet shell shading, a curved dark visor, luminous white eyes, no eyebrows, and a minimal mouth. Animate neutral → curious → delighted surprise → tired → neutral over exactly five seconds. Coordinate eye aperture, gaze, mouth, cheeks, body rotation, squash/stretch, camera pan, and zoom. Keep the background transparent and omit any ground-shadow base.

## Dark sphere emotion

> Add a 256×256 dark spherical Brot for **[emotion]**. Begin from the family's shared neutral pose, reach one unmistakable emotional peak, hold briefly, and recover. Keep the same body scale and framing as the other dark spheres. Use **[accent colors]** only for the luminous rim and reflected light.

## Realistic pale 3D orb

> Add a softly lit pale spherical Brot inspired by a tactile desktop mascot. Use inset dark eyes, subtle mouth acting, realistic radial shading, and no limbs. Animate **[emotion]** with asymmetric eye shape, a small head turn, and a restrained camera push. No floor or shadow-base ellipse.

## Floating neon mascot

> Add a rounded floating Brot in **[color]** with a glossy black visor and luminous eyes. Use a clean limb-free silhouette and a broad rounded lower curve. Animate **[emotion]** through eyes, mouth, rotation, pan, zoom, hover, and squash/stretch. Keep the five-second loop seamless and do not add hands or feet.

## Realistic antenna bot

> Add a compact 3D antenna Brot in **[color]**. Use a rounded extruded shell, glossy visor, dimensional antenna, and short rounded legs. Animate **[emotion]** through eye aperture, antenna sway, body turn, leg bounce, camera pan, and zoom. Preserve the family's shared proportions and omit any shadow base.

## Brot wordmark

> Refine the animated Brot wordmark. Keep the spacing optically compact and consistent across `B–r`, `r–o`, and `o–t`. The `o` must reuse the featured spherical multi-expression bot's shell, visor, eyes, mouth, and timing. Avoid decorative particles unless explicitly requested.
